Privacy policy

Your learning progress stays on your device.

This policy describes Simple Biology: Cell Lab’s current app design and this project website. The app is in development; optional subscriptions are being prepared for release.

Learning data and preferences

The app saves progress, notebook entries, activity statistics and preferences in local device storage. It does not send learning progress or preferences to the developer. It does not create an app account or include advertising, tracking or analytics.

Subscriptions and Apple

Optional full-curriculum subscriptions use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes purchases, renewals, refunds and restorations using your Apple account. The app receives verified product identifiers and expiry information to determine access. It does not receive payment-card details. Entitlement state is held in memory and refreshed from StoreKit; the developer does not operate a purchase server or receive your Apple-account credentials.

Learning content is bundled for offline use. An internet connection may be required to purchase, restore or refresh subscription information. Apple’s processing is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional external links

Scientific sources, support, privacy and subscription-management links open in your browser only when you choose them. The app does not send your saved learning progress with those links. The destination’s privacy practices apply.

High-school learners

The app is intended for high-school biology learners. It does not ask for a name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data. Apple may request authentication or purchase authorization in its own interface. There is no targeted advertising or behavioural tracking in the app.

Your choices

Reset learning profile clears local learning progress. Other preferences remain on the device. Deleting the app removes its local app data subject to normal operating-system backup and storage behaviour. There is no developer account or remote learning database to delete. Expired subscription access does not erase saved learning progress.

Resetting progress or deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel through Apple.

This website and support requests

This static website has no added analytics, advertising scripts, forms or cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process request information such as your IP address and browser details under GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you open a GitHub support issue, its content and your GitHub username are public. We use that information to respond and improve the project. Avoid personal, payment or student-identifying information. You control what you share through GitHub, subject to GitHub’s own storage and deletion policies.

Contact and changes

For project or policy questions, use the support page. Do not put confidential information in a public support request. We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change.
