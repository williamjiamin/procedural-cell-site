Look closer. Ask why.

Learn through observation and play.

01

Explore cell interiors

Move from the specimen view into labelled 3D teaching models. Compare structures across cells and other biological systems.

02

Investigate a process

Follow guided Stories and interactive Studies about movement, energy, transport and adaptation. Each specimen has its own biological context.

03

Question the model

Read the sources and model limits behind the activity. Colours, speeds and representative counts help explain ideas; they are not measurements.

Planned learning access

Start with the introduction.

Animal, plant and bacterial introductions will be free. The planned full-curriculum subscription includes access to the app’s 16 specimen models, 11 guided Stories and five interactive Studies.

Monthly and annual plans are being prepared. Final prices and renewal terms will be shown in the app before purchase. Subscriptions are not on sale yet.

Learning progress stays on your device. The app has no advertising or learning-data analytics.

A companion in print

The Cell, Honestly

A companion book is also in development, exploring scale, movement, cellular systems and how to read a model critically.

The book is a separate publication project. Availability and pricing have not been announced.

Curiosity comes first.

This is an educational experience with simplified models. It is not a medical tool or a predictive laboratory simulation.
