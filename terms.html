Terms of use

App licence

The planned App Store release uses Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement. That agreement governs use of the licensed app when supplied through the App Store.

Optional curriculum subscription

The introductory animal, plant and bacterial learning activities are free. A planned monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription provides full-curriculum access while active. Plans are not yet on sale. The app will display the applicable price and duration before purchase.

Apple charges your account and manages renewal. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel before renewal. You can manage or cancel it through Apple. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Educational scope

The app uses simplified biological teaching models. Colours, representative counts, dimensions, time scales and interactions may be adapted for learning. Read each model’s source and limits. The app does not provide medical advice or predict laboratory results.

Companion book

The companion book is a separate publication in development. The app subscription does not currently include a published book.
