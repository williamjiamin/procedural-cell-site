Support

Simple Biology: Cell Lab is in development. This page is the support home for the project.

Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Open a support request in the dedicated public website repository. Include the app version, device model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem.

Support requests are public. Do not post passwords, Apple account details, payment information, receipts, personal contact details or private student information. A GitHub account is required to create a request; no GitHub account is needed to use the app.

Open a support request

Restore a subscription

In the iOS app, open Full curriculum & subscription from the menu and choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple account that originally subscribed. Apple may ask you to authenticate. The free introduction remains available if no active subscription is found.

Manage billing

Apple handles subscription payments and refunds. Manage or cancel subscriptions with Apple, or request billing help from Apple. Deleting the app or resetting learning progress does not cancel a subscription.

Learning progress

Progress and preferences are saved on the device. Reset learning profile clears local learning records. The app does not currently sync progress between devices. Subscription access can be restored through Apple on supported devices.

Science and accessibility feedback

If an explanation or control is unclear, tell us which specimen, Story or Study you were using. For a science correction, include a link to the supporting source. For accessibility feedback, describe the control and the assistive feature you were using.
